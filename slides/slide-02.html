the problem

Most journaling apps die on one friction.

They ask you to type. At 11pm, tired, nobody opens an app and writes paragraphs — so the habit never sticks. The blank page wins.

the usual ask
"Open the app. Pick a prompt.
Type how you feel…"
activation energy: high · done by: almost no one
what people actually do
Tell a friend about their day
— out loud, unprompted.
activation energy: ~zero · the format we already use
