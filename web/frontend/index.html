How it works

  1. 1

    Call in, or open the web companion

    Your phone number - or your email on the web - is your identity. Nothing else to set up.

  2. 2

    Talk through your day

    Answers in the first half-second, like a friend who picks up - no spinner, no "let me look that up."

  3. 3

    Hang up - it remembers

    It quietly keeps what matters: your people, your open threads, your mood over time. Next call, it already knows you.

under the hood

A voice you'd call back has to be two things that pull against each other: instant in the first half-second, and intimate - it remembers you between calls.

Every design choice serves one of those, or the tension between them - a fast profile card on the hot path, deeper semantic memory only when you ask about the past.
